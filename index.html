AirString

Physical-Modeling Webcam Air-String Instrument

Draw glowing acoustic strings in mid-air with your hands, stretch them to bend pitch in real time, and strum them like a harp or acoustic guitar.

Two-Hand Pinch
Pinch both hands & release to anchor a new string.
Pinch Endpoint
Grab an anchor node to move, stretch & bend pitch live.
Swipe Fingers
Pluck or strum strings with all 5 fingertips (or mouse).

Mouse controls also work anytime: drag empty space to create, drag endpoints to retune, hover/swipe to strum.
